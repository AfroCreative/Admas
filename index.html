Our Story

A table that reaches
to the horizon

In Tigrinya and Amharic, admas means the horizon: the line where earth meets sky. It is the name we chose for a place where Asmara and Addis meet East Main Street.

Every plate starts with injera and ends with a conversation. Our berbere is blended in house, our butter is spiced by hand, and our stews are given the time they need. Gather around the mesob, eat with your hands, and stay for one more cup of buna.
